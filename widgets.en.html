Three simple steps

Download. Drop. Make it yours.

01

Keep the ZIP file

Download the widget package. Leave it zipped; you do not need to open the files inside.

02

Drop into Manage widgets

In Studio, open Fullscreen widgets → Manage widgets. Drag the downloaded ZIP onto the widget list, review its connection permissions and confirm.

03

Choose your settings

Select the widget and open Widget settings beside its preview. Adjust the widget options, then save. Use Show on device to open it on your screen.

The widget runs in Studio on your PC and appears on virtual DeskDeck, Android and supported ESP32 screens. No separate widget installation is needed on the phone or board. Downloading this ZIP does not update Studio itself.
