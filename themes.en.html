Three simple steps

Download. Drop. Apply.

01

Keep the ZIP file

Download the theme package without extracting it.

02

Open View → Theme

Drag the ZIP into Studio’s Theme window, or use Import to select it.

03

Preview and apply

Adjust the background and colors if you like, then choose Apply. Save theme keeps your edits in the library.

Themes change your background artwork, button colors and transparency. They apply to button screens and full-screen widgets that support themes; some widgets keep their own backgrounds.

Your existing buttons, macros and connection settings stay unchanged. The background artwork on this page was created with AI.
