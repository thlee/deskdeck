Find source materials and notices

Studio and Android product source code is private. The installer includes corresponding source for the GPL installation adapter, LGPL library source, firmware rebuild and relinking materials, and other required notices.

After installation, open Distribution/index.html in the program folder. Source materials are in Distribution/sources and DeviceEvidence, and are also available as a separate source archive in the same release. Baroham’s terms do not restrict modification, redistribution or debugging rights granted by component licenses.

Weather and city data

Korean observations use the Korea Meteorological Administration. Forecasts elsewhere use MET Norway data under CC BY 4.0. Values are rounded and weather symbols converted for display; forecasts can differ from actual observations.

The city catalog is derived from GeoNames CC BY 4.0 data, snapshot 2026-10-11, with selected fields and aliases and adjusted coordinate precision and ordering. Contains Ordnance Survey data © Crown copyright and database right 2010 and public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v1.0.

Tools not included

The Windows installer does not bundle Google Android SDK, Platform-Tools (ADB), or the UsbDk driver. Normal use does not require a separate Python installation.
