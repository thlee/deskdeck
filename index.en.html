Before you begin

A few things to know.

Do I need to buy a device?

No. Start with the virtual DeskDeck window. Click with a mouse or tap on a touch monitor. Resize it to fit a second display.

Is it free? Will I be charged later?

This public version is free, with no time limit or automatic payments. The free usage rights for the version you receive will not be retroactively converted into a paid license.

How does Android connect?

Android 5.0 or later is required. Wi-Fi on the same local network is the simplest starting point. USB AOA is also supported, but may require the optional UsbDk driver on your PC. Compatibility varies by device. USB debugging is not required for normal connections.

Why is the installer large?

It includes the Windows runtime, Android app, ESP32 firmware tools and the corresponding source and rebuild materials needed to meet third-party license terms. Normal use does not require a separate Python installation.

Can I use it at work or enter passwords?

The terms allow workplace use, but follow your organization’s security policies. Encrypted macro text is decrypted and typed into the active target window when used. DeskDeck is not a replacement for a password manager.

What about Mac and iPhone?

The public installer is for Windows x64. macOS is under development and is not distributed on this site. An iPhone app is not currently available.

Where can I ask for help?

Open a GitHub issue or email deskdeck.app@gmail.com with the app version, connection method, steps to reproduce and error message. Do not post passwords, full device backups or personal configuration files in public issues.
